NeuMark:

Robust Audio Watermarking Resistant to Neural Resynthesis


Authors: Annan Wu

Code

Abstract

Neural resynthesis (such as neural vocoders and generative speech codecs) introduces highly non-linear, time-variant distortions that easily destroy traditional audio watermarks. In this work, we propose NeuMark, a robust audio watermarking framework designed specifically to withstand neural resynthesis. By embedding watermark signals directly in the robust latent space of speech representations and utilizing differentiable distortion simulations during training, NeuMark achieves superior robustness against various generative resynthesis attacks while maintaining high perceptual transparency.
